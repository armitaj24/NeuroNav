# Orbit ✺

**A concept for making space to think through life after university.**

Orbit grew from the experience of navigating study, graduate jobs, postgraduate options and the search for mentoring at the same time. The prototype helps students put options beside one another, reflect on what matters and prepare a useful conversation with a human mentor.

## Explore the prototype

Open [index.html](index.html) in a modern browser after downloading the repository. The space scene uses the included Three.js library; the decision tools also work when 3D is unavailable.

- **Decision studio:** compare two to four options using custom criteria, importance weights and personal ratings.
- **Imagine each path:** record what could go well, what might be difficult, what to find out and one next step.
- **Mentor preparation:** copy your notes and use them to start a conversation. Links point to existing mentoring and career resources.
- **Privacy:** choices are saved in the browser on the current device, not sent to a server. Clearing browser data can remove them.

The example scenario and ratings are illustrative. Scores are a reflection of user input, not predictions or professional advice. The prototype does not provide mentoring, AI recommendations, appointments or a prediction of the future.
