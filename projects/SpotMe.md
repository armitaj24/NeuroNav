# SpotMe 🏋️

**A gym companion concept designed to help beginners feel more confident during their first 30 days.**

[Open the interactive SpotMe prototype →](https://claude.ai/artifact/18M5uzGjmrLY3wRFdNevhC)

## The idea

Starting at a gym can feel overwhelming. SpotMe explores ways to help a new member know where to go, understand equipment and build confidence through a more approachable routine.

## What's in the prototype

- An equipment guide and illustrative gym map.
- A 30-day plan and progress check-ins.
- A concept for camera-assisted exercise guidance.
- A gym dashboard concept with example member-retention trends.

The prototype uses illustrative information; it should not be interpreted as a live gym integration or a claim that every proposed feature is production-ready. The demo is not affiliated with or endorsed by Qmotion.
