# More projects ✨

## NeuroNav 🧠

A neuroscience study companion exploring how 3D visuals and small, approachable learning activities can make complex ideas easier to navigate. This repository contains a ChatGPT-assisted starter prototype.

## SpotMe 🏋️

An AI-powered gym companion concept designed to help beginners feel more confident. The project explores features such as equipment guidance, workout support and form feedback. The SpotMe team won **Best Idea at QHack 2026** at Queen Mary University of London.

SpotMe's original prototype and project link are not included in this repository yet.
